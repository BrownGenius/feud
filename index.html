Party Feud

Connect up to 4 phones in real time on GitHub Pages: Phone 1 as the PIN-protected Host Remote, Phone 2 (or laptop) cast to your TV, and Phones 3 & 4 as live podium buzzers!

🎙️ 1. Host Remote (PIN Locked)

See all rounds (including your editable survey questions & tie-breakers), reveal hidden questions to start the timer, tap to flip tiles live on the TV, trigger strikes, and manage scores.

📺 2. TV Display Screen

Open on the phone or laptop connected to the living room TV. Shows only mystery numbered tiles, live scores, and buzzer winners—never secret answers.

🔔 3 & 4. Team Podium Buzzers

Put two phones on a front table as podium buzzers. Whoever slaps their phone first triggers the TV buzzer and locks out the other team!

⚡ Single-Device / Offline Mode

Playing on one laptop/TV without separate phones? Set up your deck & teams, then launch the interactive TV board where clicking numbered tiles (or pressing keys 1–8) flips answers without spoiling the rest!
